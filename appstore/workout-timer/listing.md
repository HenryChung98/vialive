## Description (4000)

### en

A simple workout timer for intervals, rest between sets, and AMRAP workouts.

THREE TIMER TYPES
• Interval: Countdown, then Workout and Rest Time for up to 99 rounds, with an optional Cooldown
• Rest: A countdown for rest between sets, up to 10 minutes
• AMRAP: Countdown, then a workout of up to 60 minutes. Tap + to count your rounds

EASY TO FOLLOW
• Beeps for the last 5 seconds of each segment and a sound when the next one starts
• Sounds keep playing when your screen is locked or you switch apps, and they don't lower your music
• The screen turns green during workouts and blue during the cooldown
• See the current round and the total time left at a glance
• Pick a color theme in Settings

WIDGETS
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show the timer type you last selected

WORKOUT TIMER PLUS
A one-time purchase:
• Save multiple timers in a list
• Control timers from the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island (on supported iPhones): play, pause, and add rounds
• Vibration and sound volume settings
• No ads

ABOUT LIVE ACTIVITIES
• iOS turns Live Activities off after 8 hours. Just open the app to turn it back on
• While you use the buttons on the Lock Screen, iOS may turn off the display after a few seconds. This is an iOS limitation. Thank you for your understanding.
